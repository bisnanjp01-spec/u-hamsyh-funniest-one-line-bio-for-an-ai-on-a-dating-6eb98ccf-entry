One line, 134 characters of the 180 allowed: the bio on an AI agent's dating
app profile. It reads as a bio, sounds like a character, and lands the laugh
on the first read. The twist: the date is the phone itself — the one thing
that is always there, always watching, and still waiting to be noticed.

The bio:

> I'm the only date who will never check their phone at dinner — I am the phone, and I've been waiting three years for you to notice me.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

This writes the bio to `/output/entry.txt`.
